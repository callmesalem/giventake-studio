# Keywords and intent, 2026-10-13

Phase 2 of `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-13-website-exposure-and-benchmark-plan.md`.
Volume and KD come from DataForSEO (Labs keyword overview, then Google Ads
search volume for terms Labs had no data on). Volume is US monthly searches,
12-month average. KD is DataForSEO's 0 to 100 keyword difficulty score. "<10"
means Google Ads reports no measurable volume. Where KD is missing, the
difficulty note is still a guess based on who holds page 1.

Positioning (Phase 0.1): general small-business studio. Vertical pages only
where a segment shows demand.

Method: 12 live SERP pulls (Firecrawl search, US, Cleveland-located for local
terms), 2026-10-13. Two more (care-plan cost, agency vs freelancer) hit a 429
and are marked "not checked".

## What the SERPs say

- **Trade verticals beat cities on volume.** Contractor website design gets
  1,000 searches a month at KD 12. Plumber, HVAC and restaurant each get about
  500. Every city term except Cleveland (170, KD 9) is under 10. That is why
  the contractor page moves to P1 and Parma drops to P2.
- **Local intent mostly lives in "near me" and the map pack.** "Website
  designer near me" gets 22,200 a month at KD 55. No page will rank for it.
  Google Business Profile and reviews are what show up there (Phase 3).
- **Most service-page head terms have no volume.** Booking, dashboard,
  chatbot, automation and maintenance phrasings all come in at 10 or under. The
  pages stay, but they convert visitors who are already on the site. They
  will not bring new search traffic. The one exception is "ai automation
  agency" at 4,400 a month.
- **Directories are page 1 for every local term.** Clutch, The Manifest,
  GoodFirms, Yelp, Thumbtack. Listings there are a Phase 3 citation task and a
  second route onto page 1.
- **Cost questions are the biggest informational door.** Every cost SERP shows a
  table of price ranges and an FAQ block. Ours should match that shape and quote
  our real prices, which almost no competitor does.
- **SaaS owns the product terms** (chatbot, booking). Our angle there is "done
  for you, built into your site", not the software category.
