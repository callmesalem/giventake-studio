## 1. Structure of the agreement

1.1 This Agreement sets out the terms governing all services Developer provides
to Client. It does not itself commit either Party to any specific project.

1.2 Each project will be described in a separate **Statement of Work** ("**SOW**")
signed by both Parties. Each SOW incorporates this Agreement by reference.

1.3 **Order of precedence.** In the event of conflict: (a) a signed Change Order,
(b) the applicable SOW, (c) this Agreement, (d) any other document. A SOW may
vary this Agreement only where it expressly states the section being varied.

1.4 Developer's website terms of service do not govern paid engagements. This
Agreement and the applicable SOW do.

## 2. Services

2.1 Developer will perform the services described in each SOW (the "**Services**")
and deliver the items identified there (the "**Deliverables**").

2.2 Developer will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner
consistent with generally accepted industry standards.

2.3 **Independent contractor.** Developer is an independent contractor, not an
employee, partner, agent, or joint venturer of Client. Developer controls the
manner and means of performing the Services, and is responsible for its own
taxes, insurance, and equipment. Neither Party may bind the other.

2.4 **Subcontractors.** Developer may engage subcontractors provided Developer
remains responsible for their work and binds them to confidentiality and IP
assignment terms at least as protective as those in this Agreement.

## 3. AI-assisted delivery — **[REVIEW]**

> **Note:** this section is the substantive difference between this agreement and
> a conventional development contract. See
> [`../business/04-ai-delivery-policy.md`](../business/04-ai-delivery-policy.md).

3.1 **Disclosure and consent.** Client acknowledges and consents that Developer
uses artificial intelligence tools, including AI coding agents, in performing the
Services. Developer's use of such tools is described in the AI Use Disclosure
provided to Client, which Client confirms it has received and reviewed.

3.2 **Human review.** Developer will review AI-generated output before it is
delivered or deployed. Developer, not any AI system, remains responsible for the
Deliverables.

3.3 **Client material.** Developer may process Client materials through AI tools
in performing the Services. Developer will use tools configured so that Client
material is not retained for model training, and will maintain a register of such
tools available to Client on request.

3.4 **Client restrictions.** Client will notify Developer in writing before the
Effective Date of any category of Client material that may not be processed by
third-party AI tools. Absent such notice, Developer may process Client materials
as described in this Section.

3.5 **Personal data.** Where Client material includes personal data, the Parties
will execute a Data Processing Agreement, which will control over this Section as
to that data.

3.6 **Copyright limitation — [REVIEW].** Client acknowledges that the copyright
status of material generated by artificial intelligence is unsettled under United
States law and that such material may not be protectable by copyright. Developer's
assignment under Section 4 conveys all rights Developer holds, and Developer makes
no representation that exclusive copyright subsists in any AI-generated portion of
the Deliverables.

> **[REVIEW]** — This is the clause to get right. The conventional alternative
> ("Developer warrants the Deliverables are original works of authorship") may be
> false for AI-generated code, and is the warranty a client would rely on in a
> dispute. Confirm the drafting achieves: full assignment of what Developer holds,
> no warranty of exclusive copyright in AI output, and clear disclosure.

## 4. Intellectual property

4.1 **Assignment on payment.** Upon Developer's receipt of full payment for the
applicable SOW, Developer assigns to Client all right, title, and interest that
Developer holds in the Deliverables, subject to Sections 4.2 and 3.6.

4.2 **Developer background IP.** Developer retains ownership of tools, libraries,
frameworks, templates, know-how, and general methodologies that Developer owned
before the engagement or develops independently of it ("**Developer Materials**").
Where Deliverables incorporate Developer Materials, Developer grants Client a
perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, sublicensable licence to use,
modify, and distribute them as part of the Deliverables.

4.3 **Third-party and open-source components.** Deliverables may incorporate
third-party or open-source components licensed under their own terms. Developer
will identify material components and their licences on delivery.

4.4 **Prior to payment.** Until full payment, Developer retains all rights and
grants Client no licence to use the Deliverables in production.

4.5 **Client materials.** Client retains ownership of all materials it provides
and grants Developer a licence to use them solely to perform the Services.

4.6 **Reference rights — [REVIEW].** Client grants Developer the right to
identify Client as a client and to describe the Services and their results in
Developer's portfolio and marketing materials, subject to Client's prior written
approval of any specific description, not to be unreasonably withheld.

> **[REVIEW]** — Ask for this. Getting reference rights in the contract is far
> easier than requesting them after delivery, and reference material is the asset
> a new studio most needs. See
> [`../business/05-pricing-and-positioning.md`](../business/05-pricing-and-positioning.md) §6.

## 5. Fees and payment

5.1 Fees, payment schedule, and expenses are set out in each SOW.

5.2 **Deposit.** Unless a SOW states otherwise, `[50]%` of the SOW fee is due
before work begins, and the balance per the SOW schedule.

5.3 **Payment terms.** Invoices are due within `[15]` days. Late amounts accrue
interest at `[1.5]% per month` or the maximum permitted by Ohio law, whichever is
less.

5.4 **Suspension.** Developer may suspend Services on `[10]` days' written notice
of non-payment. Timeline commitments are extended by the period of suspension.

5.5 **Taxes — [REVIEW].** Fees are exclusive of applicable sales, use, and
similar taxes, which are Client's responsibility. Where required, Developer will
state such taxes separately on the invoice.

> **[REVIEW]** — Ohio's treatment of software and computer services varies by
> service type and turns on a "true object" test. Invoices must separately state
> exempt development from taxable services. See
> [`../business/02-ohio-tax.md`](../business/02-ohio-tax.md) — CPA question.

## 6. Client responsibilities

6.1 Client will provide timely access to people, systems, credentials, content,
and decisions reasonably required to perform the Services.

6.2 Client will respond to requests for feedback or approval within `[5]` business
days. Delays beyond that extend Developer's timeline commitments day for day.

6.3 Client is responsible for the accuracy and legality of materials it provides
and warrants it holds the rights to provide them.

6.4 Client will designate one individual with authority to approve scope,
deliverables, and change orders.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 Deliverables are deemed accepted on the earlier of (a) Client's written
acceptance, (b) `[10]` business days after delivery without written notice of a
material defect, or (c) Client's use of the Deliverables in production.

7.2 A "**material defect**" means a failure to conform to the acceptance criteria
in the SOW. Client's notice must identify the defect with enough specificity for
Developer to reproduce it.

7.3 Developer will correct material defects at no additional charge. Requests
that go beyond the SOW's acceptance criteria are change requests under Section 8.

## 8. Changes to scope

8.1 Changes to scope, deliverables, timeline, or fees require a written **Change
Order** signed by both Parties.

8.2 Developer will provide the fee and schedule impact of a requested change
before performing it. Developer is not obligated to perform work outside the SOW.

8.3 No verbal instruction, email, or chat message changes the scope of a SOW
unless confirmed in a signed Change Order.

## 9. Warranties and disclaimers

9.1 **Developer warrants** that: (a) it will perform the Services in a
professional and workmanlike manner; (b) it has the right to enter into this
Agreement; (c) it has run licence and provenance checks on the Deliverables using
commercially reasonable tools; and (d) it is not aware of any material in the
Deliverables that infringes a third party's rights.

9.2 **Limited remedy.** For `[30]` days after acceptance, Developer will correct
Deliverables that fail to conform to the SOW's acceptance criteria. This is
Client's exclusive remedy for defective Deliverables.

9.3 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN SECTION 9.1, THE SERVICES AND
DELIVERABLES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTIES OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR
IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND
NON-INFRINGEMENT.

9.4 **Third-party services and AI systems.** Developer does not warrant the
performance, availability, accuracy, or continued existence of third-party
services, APIs, models, or AI systems incorporated into or used to produce the
Deliverables. Such components are subject to their providers' terms.

9.5 Developer does not warrant that the Deliverables will be error-free or
uninterrupted, or that they will achieve any particular business outcome.

## 10. Limitation of liability — **[REVIEW]**

10.1 NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL,
EXEMPLARY, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, REVENUE, DATA, OR BUSINESS
OPPORTUNITY, REGARDLESS OF THE THEORY OF LIABILITY AND EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE
POSSIBILITY.

10.2 EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS
AGREEMENT AND ALL SOWs IS LIMITED TO THE FEES PAID BY CLIENT UNDER THE APPLICABLE
SOW IN THE `[12]` MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

10.3 The limitations in this Section do not apply to: (a) Client's payment
obligations; (b) either Party's breach of confidentiality; (c) Developer's
indemnity under Section 11; or (d) liability that cannot lawfully be limited,
including fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation.

> **[REVIEW]** — Set the cap at or below the bound E&O limit; never accept
> contractual liability exceeding what insurance will pay. Watch for client
> redlines that leave 10.2 intact but add an uncapped indemnity — same effect,
> different clause. See
> [`../business/03-insurance-and-risk.md`](../business/03-insurance-and-risk.md).

## 11. Indemnification — **[REVIEW]**

11.1 **By Developer.** Developer will defend and indemnify Client against
third-party claims that the Deliverables, as delivered and excluding Client
Materials and third-party components, infringe a United States copyright, trade
secret, or patent, provided Client promptly notifies Developer, gives Developer
control of the defence, and cooperates. Developer's obligation under this Section
is subject to the cap in Section 10.2.

11.2 **By Client.** Client will defend and indemnify Developer against
third-party claims arising from Client Materials, Client's use of the
Deliverables in violation of law, or Client's breach of Section 6.3.

11.3 **Exclusions.** Developer has no obligation for claims arising from
modifications not made by Developer, combination with materials not supplied by
Developer, or Client's continued use after notice to stop.

> **[REVIEW]** — Whether to give an IP indemnity at all, and whether it can be
> capped, is a business decision to make with the attorney and broker together.
> Confirm your E&O responds to contractual indemnities and does not exclude IP
> infringement claims.

## 12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each Party will protect the other's non-public information disclosed in
connection with this Agreement with at least reasonable care, use it only to
perform or receive the Services, and not disclose it except to personnel and
subcontractors bound by comparable obligations.

12.2 Excluded: information that is or becomes public without breach, was known
before disclosure, is independently developed, or is rightfully received from a
third party.

12.3 Disclosure required by law is permitted with prompt notice where lawful.

12.4 Obligations survive `[3]` years after termination, and indefinitely for
trade secrets.

## 13. Data protection and security

13.1 Developer will apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to
protect Client data, including encryption in transit, access controls, and
least-privilege credential handling.

13.2 Developer will notify Client without undue delay and in any event within
`[72]` hours of becoming aware of a security incident affecting Client data.

13.3 Where Developer processes personal data on Client's behalf, the Parties will
execute a Data Processing Agreement.

13.4 On termination, Developer will return or destroy Client data on request,
except copies required by law or retained in routine backups.

## 14. Term and termination

14.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated.

14.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on `[30]` days'
written notice, provided that any SOW in progress continues under its own terms
unless separately terminated.

14.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for material breach not
cured within `[15]` days of written notice.

14.4 **On termination of a SOW**, Client will pay for all Services performed and
expenses incurred through the termination date. Developer will deliver
work-in-progress on payment. **Rights in Deliverables transfer only for
Deliverables paid in full.**

14.5 **Retainer SOWs** may be terminated by either Party on `[30]` days' written
notice effective at the end of a billing month. Fees for the notice period remain
payable.

14.6 Sections 4, 9.3–9.5, 10, 11, 12, 13.4, and 15 survive termination.

## 15. General

15.1 **Governing law and venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the
State of Ohio, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. The exclusive venue
for any dispute is the state and federal courts located in **Cuyahoga** County,
Ohio, and each Party consents to personal jurisdiction there.

> Cuyahoga is the county of the entity's address of record (Parma). Confirm it is
> also where you operate from before this is final.

15.2 **Dispute resolution — [REVIEW].** Before filing suit, the Parties will
attempt good-faith resolution through their designated representatives for
`[30]` days.

> **[REVIEW]** — Consider whether to add binding arbitration. Arbitration is
> generally faster and cheaper for small disputes and keeps them private, but
> forecloses appeal. A reasonable middle ground is arbitration for disputes below
> a threshold and litigation above it.

15.3 **Non-solicitation.** During the term and for `[12]` months afterward,
neither Party will knowingly solicit for employment any individual who performed
work under this Agreement, excluding general public advertising.

15.4 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay caused by events beyond
its reasonable control, provided it gives prompt notice and resumes as soon as
practicable.

15.5 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's
written consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all
assets.

15.6 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing to the addresses above or by email to
the addresses designated in the SOW, and are effective on receipt.

15.7 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, together with all SOWs and Change
Orders, is the entire agreement between the Parties on this subject and
supersedes all prior discussions, proposals, and marketing materials.

15.8 **Amendment.** Only by a writing signed by both Parties.

15.9 **Severability.** If a provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in
effect and the provision is modified to the minimum extent necessary.

15.10 **Waiver.** No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no waiver of one
breach waives any other.

15.11 **Counterparts.** May be executed in counterparts and by electronic
signature.
